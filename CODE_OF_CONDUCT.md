# Code of conduct

Be respectful, constructive, and welcoming across experience levels and backgrounds. Discuss the work and support disagreements with evidence. Respect privacy and the rights of people whose images or data appear in examples.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, personal attacks, and publishing someone's private information without permission are unacceptable. These expectations apply to project issues, pull requests, and other project spaces, and when representing the project publicly.

Use GitHub's **Report content** action on abusive content, and contact the repository maintainer privately through the contact options on their GitHub profile when appropriate. Do not publish sensitive details in an issue. Maintainers may remove content, limit participation, or block contributors, depending on the severity and repetition of the behavior. Reports will be handled with discretion.

Maintainers are subject to these expectations too. Reports concerning a maintainer can be sent directly to [GitHub Support](https://support.github.com/).
